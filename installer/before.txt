PiXELL-River Account Manager - Installation Preparation

Thank you for choosing PiXELL-River Account Manager!

Before proceeding with installation, please ensure:

1. SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS:
   - Operating System: Windows 10 or newer (64-bit)

   - Screen Resolution: 1024x768 minimum

3. INSTALLATION NOTES:
